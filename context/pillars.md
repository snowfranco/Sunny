# Content pillars

Every piece of content maps to exactly one of these three pillars. The
angle engine tags each angle with its pillar; the reviewer checks the match.

## 1. Build-in-public, PM lens (`build_in_public`)

Real shipped projects narrated as product decisions, including the calls
that were wrong. The unit of content is a decision and what it cost or
taught, not a feature announcement. Wrong turns are part of the story, told
as "that didn't work out as expected, but here's what I learned."

## 2. AI for people who ship, not just consume (`ai_for_shippers`)

Practical AI workflows and tooling for operators and small teams. The test
for this pillar: could a reader take this and change how they work this
week? Written from actual use, not from reading release notes.

## 3. The operator's transition story (`transition_story`)

Fifteen years across four countries, studying while building, the pivot
itself. Career arc as material: SaaS delivery across eCommerce, telecoms,
legal, and B2B, now an AI-focused PM building freelance income and a
portfolio mid-degree. The cross-cultural, remote-first thread belongs here.

## Explicitly not a pillar

Generic AI news roundups. News is fuel inside a pillar, never a standalone
post, with one deliberate exception: the Portal Technologies / Frameshift
briefing format (see `platforms.md`), which is a curated signal report by
design. Even there, the value is Snow's editorial framing and item
commentary, not the aggregation itself.
